Our Story

A Gathering Place
at the Heart of Town

The Hubber Cafe is Smethport's favorite spot to slow down, reconnect, and enjoy something delicious. Whether you're swinging by for your morning coffee, sitting down for a hearty lunch, or catching up with a friend over a slice of homemade pie — you're always welcome here.

We take pride in fresh, made-from-scratch cooking, friendly faces, and the kind of atmosphere that makes you feel right at home. Because for us, this isn't just a cafe — it's the hub of our community.
